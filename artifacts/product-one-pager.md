# CloudPilot OS — Product One-Pager

**AI-native CloudOps command center for enterprises and MSPs**

---

## The Problem

Cloud teams are drowning in operational toil. With 5–12 disconnected tools, reactive workflows, and manual reporting, they can't keep pace with the complexity of modern cloud environments.

The symptoms:
- Cost anomalies discovered weeks late — after the bill arrives
- Security misconfigurations aging for months in backlogs
- Compliance evidence collected manually for 4 weeks before every audit
- MSPs unable to scale past 5 accounts per engineer
- CloudOps engineers spending 40% of time on routine monitoring

---

## The Solution

CloudPilot OS is a single AI-native operating platform for Day-2 cloud operations. It connects to cloud accounts and deploys specialized AI agents that continuously monitor, detect, recommend, and act — with human oversight and full auditability.

**What it does:**
- Unifies cloud inventory, cost, security, and compliance in one platform
- AI agents run continuously: detecting anomalies, generating recommendations, drafting remediations
- Governed approval workflows: humans approve before agents execute
- AI-generated reports for executives, engineers, and auditors
- MSP portfolio view: manage 50 customers from one screen

---

## Trust Model

Agents earn autonomy incrementally:
- **Level 1 (default):** Recommendations only
- **Level 3 (30 days):** Execute on approval
- **Level 4 (90 days):** Auto-execute pre-approved low-risk actions

Every action is logged, explainable, and reversible.
